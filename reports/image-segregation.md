# Image segregation

The six reviewed inscriptions contain 106 nonseparator glyph occurrences. All 106 occur exactly once in the inventory and once in the shape group partition. There are 49 groups at the chosen 86 percent complete link threshold. This number is a shape grouping result, not a count of letters in an alphabet.

The right border final glyph is U001 and remains unassigned. U002 and U003 are two tablet graphics; their status as letters is unverified. The rest retain previous Russian and English crib annotations. Grouping does not establish those readings independently.

## Comparisons

137 reference forms cover the existing independent Bill English chart, Noto Runic forms, and 22 chart slots each for Celestial, Malachim and Passing the River. Historical slots come from the alphabet images associated with Agrippa Book III chapter 30 in the Esoteric Archives edition. The slot cuts are provisional and no Hebrew letter values are assigned. Reference glyph thumbnails and scores are available inside the dictionary. Theban is a historical source lead only and was not added to the automated bank.

The score averages the fraction of black pixels in each normalized mask falling within one pixel of black pixels in the other. Masks are independently trimmed and stretched to 24 by 32. This tolerates handwriting differences but can confuse simple geometric forms. Scores are not identity probabilities, have no calibrated acceptance threshold, and never produce a new automatic translation. Only upright crops are tested here.

## Limits

This is a bounded inventory of six inscriptions and two tablet graphics. Background drawings, faint signatures, and tiny unresolved decorative marks are not claimed to be exhaustively segmented. No additional language, cipher family, calendar interpretation or book origin has been confirmed.
