Four passages reread in context. Arithmetic and geometric tests assess candidate rules, not plaintext or credentials. Artwork attribution and provisional glyph readings remain unverified.

The strongest anchor remains the exact phrase in Chapter 10. The passages suggest timing, alternation and reversal, but none supplies all the choices required for a reproducible glyph reading. No recovered message is claimed.

Chapter 10 Clocks and public judgment

Context: The chapter opens with 4,000 synchronized clocks at 2:27. The Director arranges a public confrontation at 2:30 and speaks with Henry Foster before condemning Bernard. The exact artwork phrase occurs in that accusation.

Candidate: Use the phrase to select Chapter 10, then treat its clocks as an orientation or timing clue.

Test: 2:27 is 147 minutes after noon; 2:30 is 150. The difference is three minutes. The artwork has a clock motif, but no verified marking selects either time or the difference.

Result: Specific contextual lead; no unique numerical instruction. Taking the nearest number or adding numbers would introduce a rule absent from the passage.

Chapter 5 Alternation around the circle

Context: Twelve chairs surround a circular table. Participants alternate by sex. The cup passes from Morgana to Bernard to Clara, providing an explicit local order. The chapter also specifies alternate Thursdays, floor 33 and Room 3210.

Candidate: Read alternating positions in the artwork clock or a circular glyph sequence.

Test: A twelve position ring has two parity classes of six positions each. Alternation alone does not choose a class, a starting position or a direction. The named cup sequence fixes local order in the story, but no artwork anchor identifies those people.

Result: A plausible operation with missing anchors. Do not silently discard Thursdays, floor 33 or Room 3210 while treating twelve as uniquely selected.

Chapter 7 The tower and both weekdays

Context: Linda remembers a pink glass tower at the Chelsea Abortion Centre, illuminated on Tuesdays and Fridays. It is distinct from the Charing T Tower comparison earlier in the chapter.

Candidate: Use the provisional Tuesday reading near the artwork tower to select this scene or a weekday order.

Test: With Monday first, Tuesday and Friday are positions 2 and 5. With Sunday first they are 3 and 6. Their linear separation is three in either convention, but neither convention is specified. The artwork Tuesday reading remains a community crib, not independent proof.

Result: The two weekdays are supported together. A Tuesday only extraction omits source evidence. The novel tower is in Chelsea, not Seattle.

Chapter 18 Compass movement and reversal

Context: The final scene describes the movement of John’s feet after his death, comparing them with compass needles. They turn right through six named bearings, pause and return left through four bearings. This is a tragic description, not an explicit instruction to the reader.

Candidate: Apply the indicated directional sequence to a radial artwork feature.

Test: Using north as zero and clockwise positive gives 0, 45, 90, 135, 180, 202.5 degrees, followed by 202.5, 180, 135, 90. The return is a partial reversal with a repeated endpoint. A standard twelve position clock uses 30 degree increments; 45, 135 and 202.5 do not land on that grid. All bearings fit a sixteen point compass, but the artwork has no established sixteen point anchor.

Result: Reject a direct twelve position clock mapping without added interpolation. Preserve south-south-west and the repeated endpoint; replacing it with southwest changes the source.
